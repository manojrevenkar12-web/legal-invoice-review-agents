# Lindeveld Energie N.V. — Outside Counsel Engagement and Billing Guidelines

Issued by the Legal & Compliance Department (Legal Operations), Arnhem, the Netherlands. Version 1, applicable from 1 January 2027.

Lindeveld Energie operates electricity and gas distribution networks in Gelderland and Overijssel and supplies energy to around 1.4 million households and businesses. As a regulated network operator we are accountable to the ACM, to our shareholders (the provinces and municipalities we serve) and ultimately to the people who pay our tariffs. We therefore ask our external advisers to work with the same care for cost that we expect of ourselves. These Guidelines explain how we instruct counsel, what we will and will not pay for, and how invoices must be presented.

## 1. Application

1.1 These Guidelines form part of every engagement between Lindeveld Energie N.V. or any of its subsidiaries ("Lindeveld", "we") and any law firm, notary practice or other legal adviser ("the Firm", "counsel") instructed by the Legal & Compliance Department. They apply to all work performed on or after 1 January 2027, including work on matters opened before that date.

1.2 If an engagement letter conflicts with these Guidelines, these Guidelines prevail unless the engagement letter expressly identifies the provision being departed from and has been countersigned by the General Counsel or the Head of Legal Operations.

1.3 Nothing in these Guidelines is intended to limit counsel's professional judgement or its obligations under the Dutch Bar Association's rules of conduct (Gedragsregels advocatuur) or any equivalent rules in another jurisdiction. Where you consider that compliance with a provision would conflict with those duties, please tell the Responsible Lawyer straight away.

1.4 Each matter will have a named in-house Responsible Lawyer. Only the Responsible Lawyer, the General Counsel or the Head of Legal Operations may give the approvals referred to in these Guidelines, and approvals must be in writing (e-mail is sufficient).

## 2. Engagement and Matter Management

2.1 No work may begin until the Firm has received a matter number from Legal Operations. Work performed before a matter number is issued should be recorded and carried over to the matter once it is opened; please do not invoice it under a general or "miscellaneous" reference.

2.2 Before accepting an instruction, the Firm must run a full conflict check against Lindeveld Energie N.V., its subsidiaries and the counterparties we identify, and confirm the result in writing. Lindeveld does not give general advance waivers. Any potential conflict that arises during a matter must be disclosed promptly.

2.3 For any matter expected to exceed EUR 25,000 in fees, the Firm should submit, within thirty days of instruction, a written estimate broken down by phase, together with its assumptions and the proposed team.

2.4 Fees and disbursements in excess of the approved budget for a matter, or for any phase of it, will be paid only if the Responsible Lawyer has approved a revised budget in writing before the additional cost was incurred.

2.5 We appreciate a short written status update at the end of each calendar quarter on every open matter, covering progress, next steps, expected spend and any change in our risk assessment. Early warning of developments that may affect the outcome or the cost is always welcome.

2.6 Lindeveld must be consulted before any significant step is taken in a matter, including the issue of proceedings, the filing of any substantive pleading, the making or acceptance of a settlement proposal, and any communication with a regulator.

## 3. Staffing

3.1 We expect matters to be staffed lean and at the right level of seniority. Work should be delegated to the most junior timekeeper who can perform it competently, and partners should concentrate on strategy, key judgement calls and client contact.

3.2 The team proposed at the outset should remain stable. Please let the Responsible Lawyer know in advance of any change to the core team, and explain the reason.

3.3 Time recorded by student interns or by the firm's law librarians is not chargeable to Lindeveld, whatever the nature of the work they carry out.

3.4 Lindeveld will pay for attendance at hearings and examinations only to the following extent:

(a) at any court or arbitral hearing, including oral argument before the College van Beroep voor het bedrijfsleven, the fees of no more than two timekeepers will be paid;

(b) for depositions in proceedings in the United States or other jurisdictions that use them, only one lawyer's attendance will be paid, whether the Firm is taking or defending the deposition.

3.5 Lindeveld is committed to an inclusive workplace and supplier base. We ask our panel firms to share our commitment, to give lawyers of diverse backgrounds meaningful roles on our matters, and to report once a year on the composition of the teams that work for us.

3.6 Secondments of Firm lawyers to our in-house team are agreed separately and are not governed by Sections 4 to 7.

## 4. Fees and Rates

4.1 Time may be charged only at the hourly rate agreed in writing for each individual timekeeper. Any entry charged at a higher rate, or by a timekeeper for whom no rate has been agreed, will be paid at the agreed rate or, where there is none, not at all.

4.2 Requests to adjust agreed rates should be submitted to Legal Operations by 1 October in order to be considered for the following calendar year. We do not accept rate changes notified by means of an invoice.

4.3 We are open to fixed fees, capped fees, success components and other alternative fee arrangements, and we encourage the Firm to propose them where a matter lends itself to one.

## 5. Recording and Describing Time

5.1 Time is to be recorded in units of one-tenth of an hour, reflecting the time actually spent. Minimum charges per task (for example a standard quarter of an hour for reading an e-mail) are not accepted.

5.2 Every time entry must identify the timekeeper, the date, the time spent and a description of the specific work done, sufficient for a reader without knowledge of the file to understand what was done and why. Descriptions such as "attention to file", "work on matter", "review documents", "correspondence", "research" or "telephone call" without further detail do not meet this standard, and time recorded under them will not be paid.

5.3 Each task must be shown as a separate entry with its own time. Where an entry groups two or more tasks under a single time charge, Lindeveld will pay seventy-five per cent of the time so recorded.

5.4 Lindeveld will not pay for more than ten hours recorded by any one timekeeper on any calendar day, unless the Responsible Lawyer has agreed beforehand that a hearing or an urgent application justifies it.

5.5 Conferences, meetings, calls and e-mail exchanges between timekeepers of the Firm are part of the normal running of a matter. Where they are necessary, only one participant may charge for them; the time of every other participant will be written off.

5.6 Administrative and clerical activities are part of the Firm's overhead and may not be charged, regardless of who performs them. These include opening and closing files, diary management, scanning, copying, collating and indexing documents, arranging meetings and travel, proof-reading for typographical errors and preparing or correcting invoices.

5.7 Legal research will be paid for up to a total of eight hours per matter in any calendar month. Research going beyond that requires the Responsible Lawyer's prior approval. We do not expect to pay for research into points of law that an experienced practitioner in the relevant field ought to know.

5.8 Travel time during which no work is done on a Lindeveld matter is chargeable at half of the timekeeper's agreed hourly rate. Time spent working on the matter while travelling may be charged in the ordinary way, but must be described as such.

5.9 We value the Firm's know-how and would like it to be shared with our in-house team. Short calls from our lawyers asking for a quick view on a point are, in our experience, best handled informally, and we would ask the relationship partner to discuss with us how such calls are to be dealt with.

## 6. Disbursements

6.1 We reimburse reasonable disbursements properly incurred on a matter, at cost and without any surcharge. Please attach copies of receipts for every item above EUR 75.

6.2 The following are treated as the Firm's overhead and will not be reimbursed: word processing, typing and other secretarial services; telephone, mobile and conference-call charges; facsimile transmissions; subscription or usage charges for online legal research services such as Legal Intelligence, Kluwer Navigator, Westlaw or LexisNexis; and taxis, parking and other local transport within the Firm's home city.

6.3 Photocopying and printing carried out within the Firm may be charged at a maximum of EUR 0.10 per page. For large copying jobs we prefer an external provider at a price agreed in advance.

6.4 Meals taken in the course of necessary overnight travel are reimbursed up to EUR 40 per person per meal.

6.5 Travel arrangements should be booked with cost in mind. Wherever practical within the Netherlands and to neighbouring countries we ask counsel to travel by train, in line with Lindeveld's own sustainability policy.

6.6 Court fees (griffierechten) and bailiff costs are generally paid by Lindeveld directly. Please agree with the Responsible Lawyer in advance whether a particular fee will be advanced by the Firm.

## 7. Invoices

7.1 Invoices must be addressed to Lindeveld Energie N.V., attn. Legal Operations, Postbus 9150, 6800 KA Arnhem, and submitted electronically through our e-billing platform. Paper invoices and invoices sent by e-mail to individual lawyers will not be processed.

7.2 Invoices are to be submitted in LEDES 1998B format, with every fee entry coded to the UTBMS litigation or counselling task and activity codes and every disbursement coded to the UTBMS expense codes.

7.3 Each invoice must reach us within sixty days after the end of the month in which the work was performed. Invoices received after that period will not be accepted for payment.

7.4 Please render a separate invoice for each matter, quote our matter number and purchase order number, and show VAT separately at the applicable rate. Where the reverse-charge mechanism applies, the invoice must say so.

7.5 If we dispute part of an invoice, we will pay the undisputed portion within our standard payment term of thirty days and notify the Firm of the disputed items with our reasons. The Firm may respond within twenty-one days; we will then try to resolve the matter promptly and in good faith.

7.6 Legal Operations is the first point of contact for any invoicing question. Please do not raise invoicing queries with the Responsible Lawyer.

## 8. Confidentiality, Data Protection and Security

8.1 All information received from or about Lindeveld, its customers and its network is confidential and may be used only for the purpose of the engagement. Particular care is required with information about critical infrastructure, which may be shared within the Firm only with those who need it for the matter.

8.2 To the extent that the Firm processes personal data on our behalf, it does so in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (AVG) and the Dutch Implementation Act (UAVG), and, where we so request, under a data processing agreement in our standard form. Personal data of our customers must not be transferred outside the European Economic Area without our prior written consent.

8.3 Any actual or suspected security incident affecting Lindeveld information must be reported to our Security Operations Centre within twenty-four hours of discovery, even if the investigation is not yet complete.

8.4 The Firm may not refer to Lindeveld or to any matter in marketing materials, directory submissions, press statements or on social media without our prior written consent.

8.5 Counsel are expected to observe Lindeveld's Supplier Code of Conduct, which covers integrity, anti-bribery, human rights and environmental responsibility, and to tell us if they become aware of any breach of it in connection with our work.

## 9. Review and Amendments

9.1 Lindeveld, or an auditor it appoints, may review the Firm's time records, disbursement receipts and other supporting documents for any matter for up to five years after the final invoice on that matter. The Firm will co-operate with any such review.

9.2 We will review these Guidelines periodically and will give panel firms at least one month's notice of any amendment. Questions about these Guidelines should be directed to Legal Operations at legal.operations@lindeveld-energie.example.

Thank you for your co-operation. We look forward to working with you.
